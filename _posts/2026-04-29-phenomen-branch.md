I continue to develop the [phenomenological branch](https://k-osmolovskyi.github.io/#phenomenology-branch), although at the moment I do not see much direct practical significance in it.

I am doing this simply because I can, and because this area is becoming distinguishable to me now.

The structure and function of emotions and sensations have already taken shape and are now described in prose. As expected, sensations and emotions appear to function as integral markers of the state of a system and of its dynamics. Coherence evaluation, from which this line of thought originally began to unfold, is directly related to this.

In my view, it would not be correct to say that a system processes only what is marked by emotions or sensations. First, a large part of processing takes place through automatisms. Second, emotions already belong to the regulation of more complex structures. However, it is already possible to say with confidence that an emotion is an integral marker of the system’s state, indicating something like: “direct attention here; something is not working properly,” or, conversely: “direct attention here; this is working well.”

For now, the phenomenological branch is describing in prose. This form of presentation is sometimes confusing, but once I have fixed the area itself, I will gradually formalize each node in more detail. The v2 versions will be cleaner, with clear formulas rather than densely connected prose.
