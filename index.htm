Why This Framework Exists

Most models assume full observability, error-driven adaptation, or continuous plasticity. Real systems—biological, artificial, or institutional—operate under bounded resources, historical constraints, and selective access. This series formalizes what happens when structural tension accumulates, when discrepancies are filtered before representation, and when identity emerges not as a self-model, but as a regulatory attractor.

  • Layered Admissibility: Geometry ≠ Accessibility ≠ Representation ≠ Structural Updating
  • Trajectory Dependence: Identical instantaneous states differ regulatorily due to overload memory
  • Substrate Independence: Applicable to neural, algorithmic, and social architectures
  • Open & Verifiable: Axioms, theorems, proofs, and minimal code openly published
